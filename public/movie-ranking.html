The Best Movies According to Esma

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Moulin Rouge

An incredible musical masterpiece filled with breathtaking visual aesthetics and deep romance. The emotional depth and vibrant energy make it completely unforgettable.

Edward Scissorhands

A profoundly touching fantasy tale about isolation, gentleness, and misunderstood beauty. Tim Burton's magical atmosphere creates a truly timeless classic.

The Exorcist (1973)

A cinematic masterpiece of psychological and supernatural horror that changed the genre forever. Its haunting tension and brilliant storytelling are unmatched.
